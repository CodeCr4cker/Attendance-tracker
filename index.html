Attendance calculator

How this works

If you're below target, this works out the fewest additional classes you'd need to attend consecutively to reach it. If you're above target, it works out how many more classes you could miss in a row and still stay at or above your target. Both use your current totals as the starting point — auto-filled from your live records if you open this page after logging attendance.

User guide

Quick start

  1. Add your subjects in Subjects. If you already have past attendance, enter it as the opening balance.
  2. Open Attendance, pick Class or Exam, a subject, a date and a status, then save.
  3. Check the Dashboard and Reports for percentages, and how many classes you can miss.

Manual features

  • Whole-day marking: tick "Apply to all subjects" on the form, or tap a date in the Calendar and choose "Mark day as holiday / suspended".
  • Several classes a day: save the same subject again on the same date to add another class.
  • Exams: choose Entry type "Exam". Exam attendance is shown separately and only joins your overall percentage if enabled in Settings.
  • Substitution: record who took the class and whether you attended.
  • Period and note: optional details, shown in history, reports and CSV.
  • Future dates: allowed only for holiday, suspended, cancelled and event entries.
  • Timetable: choose your semester (1 to 8) and add weekly lecture slots and an exam timetable. When a lecture or exam ends, attendance is recorded automatically (Present by default). Edit any record you missed; your own manual entries and holidays are never overwritten.
  • College holidays: add festivals or breaks (single day or a range) on the Timetable page. They are recorded as Holiday for all subjects automatically, and replace any timetable auto-marked attendance for those days. Your own manual entries are kept.
  • Weekends: Saturday and Sunday are added as holidays automatically at 9:00 a.m. (configurable in Settings). Edit the record, or log attendance beforehand, to make that day a working day. Records are added when Ledger is open, and any missed weekend is added the next time you open it.
  • Rules: in Settings, decide whether late, on-duty and medical leave count as present.
  • Edit or delete: use the pencil and bin icons in Recent entries or History.
